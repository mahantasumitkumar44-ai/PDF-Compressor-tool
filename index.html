Standard Document Specifications for Competitive Exam Applications

  • SSC (CGL, CHSL, GD, MTS): Recent passport photograph must be between 20 KB and 50 KB (JPG/JPEG), and signature between 10 KB and 20 KB.
  • UPSC & State PSC Portals: Clear photograph and signature on a plain light background, typically required between 20 KB and 300 KB.
  • Academic & Category Certificates: Marksheets, caste certificates, and domicile documents are generally uploaded in PDF format under 100 KB to 500 KB.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I resize a passport photo to 20KB–50KB for SSC or UPSC forms?

Select the Exact KB Photo / Sign tool, upload your JPG or PNG photo, click the SSC / UPSC Photo (45 KB) preset, and press Compress & Resize Image. Your resized image is processed instantly inside your browser.

Are my photos, signatures, and certificates uploaded to a server?

No. FormReady runs 100% client-side using HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. Your personal documents never leave your device.

How can I add my Name and Date of Photo (DOP) to an exam photograph?

Open the Photo + Name & Date tab, upload your passport photograph, enter your full name and date of photo, and download the formatted image with a clean white bottom strip.

How do I compress an existing multi-page PDF under 200KB?

Go to the Compress Existing PDF tab, upload your PDF file, set 200 KB as the target limit, and click Compress PDF File.
